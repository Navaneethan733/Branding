Founding

Build brands that hold up long after we've handed over the files.

Really what lies between the most creative work's experience and passive value is the policy and the ability to act on. We drive strategy, design identity, and build the digital experience — the full brand stack — so nothing important left behind to be discovered, shared, it's built.

Ten years, one layer at a time.

  1. 2015

    Founded as a two-person design studio

    Solving naming gaps and mismatched visuals for scale-up founders in Berlin.

  2. 2017

    First embedded position taken

    Directly delivering work alongside in-house teams — our first full-design-as-a-service contract.

  3. 2020

    Web & digital design practice launched

    Added the third practice to take strategy into actual products that people use.

  4. 2022

    Brand systems practice formalized

    Built the governance layer so brands keep quality without us in every room.

  5. 2024

    10 specialists, 12 countries

    120 brands shipped. Same promise, bigger reach.
