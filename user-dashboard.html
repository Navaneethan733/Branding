Brand Usage Rules & Compliance

Core standards for marketing collateral, co-branding, and partners.

Recommended Usage
  • Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the logo mark 'S'.
  • Display primary logo mark on dark obsidian backgrounds (#0D0E12).
  • Use vector SVG assets exclusively for high-density web displays.
Prohibited Variations
  • Do not stretch, skew, or rotate the logomark at non-standard angles.
  • Do not apply unapproved gradient dropshadows or heavy bevel filters.
  • Do not pair the logo mark with unverified third-party color palettes.
